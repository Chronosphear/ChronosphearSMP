SERVER RULES

Respect the community and help keep Chronosphear enjoyable.

01

Respect Everyone

Treat all players and staff with respect. Harassment and discrimination are not allowed.

02

No Cheating

Hacked clients, unfair advantages, and exploit abuse are not permitted.

03

No Griefing

Do not destroy, steal from, or damage another player's builds without permission.

04

Listen to Staff

Follow reasonable instructions from server staff and respect their decisions.

05

No Spam

Avoid chat flooding, advertising, and disruptive behavior.

06

Have Fun

Enjoy the server, play fairly, and help make the community better.
